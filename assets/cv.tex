    \section{Biography}
    
My research focuses on Real2Sim and Sim2Real for embodied agents.

In Real2Sim, I aim to leverage spatial intelligence models with affordable sensing systems to address generalizable human and scene understanding, enabling the acquisition of high-quality, simulation-ready data without tele-operation.


In Sim2Real, I am interested in developing behavior foundation models for robust motor control, while using world models for long-horizon planning and environment modeling, toward more general intelligence for humanoid robots.
