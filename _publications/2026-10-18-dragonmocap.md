DragonMoCap recovers dragon motion from monocular video using language-grounded joint queries that combine semantic and skeletal information. Non-parametric query-token retrieval enables zero-shot adaptation to unseen rigs without retraining, supporting diverse dragon topologies and production animation workflows.
